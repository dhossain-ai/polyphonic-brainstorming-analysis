Session: 02
Type: Human-human
Topic: Can a machine be creative?
Participants: StudentA, StudentB

StudentA: I saw AI-generated art online, and some of it looked very creative.
StudentB: I agree it can look creative, but I am not sure the machine itself is creative.
StudentA: Why not? If the output is new and interesting, maybe that is enough.
StudentB: Because the machine follows patterns from data. It does not have personal ideas.
StudentA: Humans also learn from examples before making something new.
StudentB: Yes, but humans have emotions, memories, and goals.
StudentA: Maybe creativity has different levels. AI can have technical creativity.
StudentB: That makes sense. It can combine things in unexpected ways.
StudentA: Also, AI can help humans brainstorm faster.
StudentB: Yes, but it may repeat common ideas and not take real risks.
StudentA: So AI may be useful as a creative tool, not a full creative person.
StudentB: I agree. The creativity may be shared between the human user and the machine.
